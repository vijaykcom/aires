01 / THE GLOBAL CHALLENGE

Food waste is a global problem.

Nearly ⅓

of the food produced worldwide is wasted.

Why does waste happen?

People often cannot accurately judge whether food is still fresh or has started to spoil.

What can happen?

Perfectly edible food may be thrown away, while spoiled food may sometimes be accidentally consumed.

06 / PROBLEM → SOLUTION → IMPACT

Small information can support better choices.

🗑️ THE PROBLEM

Food waste is a global challenge, and people may not know whether food is still fresh.

💡 OUR SOLUTION

A reusable chip and colour indicators communicate the proposed freshness state.

🌱 THE IMPACT
